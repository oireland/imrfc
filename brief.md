## Context
I am 1st XV Captain for my Univeristy rugby team - Imperial Medicals RFC (IMRFC)
We have a problem of keeping track of where equipment is, and who is responsible for bringing what to training and matches.
This has resulted in equipment being forgotten many times before.
Another issue is having no centralised place where dates/times for upcoming events can be found.
Both of these issues result in repeatedly asking via Whatsapp the same questions

## Goal
To build a system for seeing upcoming events, tracking where equipment is, and assigning people to take equipment to events like trianing and matches.
The "Upcoming Events" can be training, matches, and social events.
For kit tracking, there are two separate "views" - where equipment is stored, and who is taking it to/from an event.

## Tech
This needs to be free to build. We'll make a nextjs app and host it on vercel. 
We'll need a database that is free hosted as well.
Need authentication and a way for admins to block users (e.g. someone from a rival club tries to sign up and mess things up)

## Future extension
The system must be composed in a way that it can have more functionality added easily in the future

No need to worry about vercel deployments until we have the product fully functional locally
